| Day 1 | Publish one intro post on X. | Baseline impressions and profile visits. |
| Day 2 | Publish one intro post on Bluesky with screenshot. | Compare tone and test whether media improves profile visits. |
| Day 3 | Reply/comment from the account for 15 minutes. | Make the account look alive without spam. |

| Day 6 | Review metrics and keep the best-performing wording. | Learn before scaling. |

## Manual Publish Safety

- Read the post once out loud before publishing.
- Check every link opens in a private browser window.
- Use the main website URL in account profiles, but use the devlog URL in individual posts when the copy points to a specific first look or dev note.
- Do not add hashtags if they push X over 280 characters.
- Do not mention Steam until the Steam page exists.
- Do not mention a demo, release date, or system requirements until confirmed.
- Screenshot posts should show actual Kalma visuals, not placeholder art.
